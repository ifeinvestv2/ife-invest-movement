WHAT IS IFE INVEST?

From money sent home to possibilities built together.

IFE means love in Yoruba. (I)ndustry · (F)inance · (E)quity describes how we bring that purpose to life. Through three simple actions — SEND, SAVE and INVEST — we’re creating a clearer connection to African enterprise and opportunity. We care about who owns Africa’s future, and we believe you should have a chance to be part of it.
