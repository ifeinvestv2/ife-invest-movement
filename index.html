WHAT IS IFE INVEST?

From money sent home to possibilities built together.

IFE means love in Yoruba. (I)ndustry · (F)inance · (E)quity describes how we bring that purpose to life. Through three simple actions — SEND, SAVE and INVEST — we’re creating a clearer connection to African enterprise and opportunity. We care about who owns Africa’s future, and we believe you should have a chance to be part of it.

01 · SEND

Send with purpose

We plan to make sending money home simpler through regulated partners, connecting remittances to longer-term goals.

02 · SAVE

Save for what matters

Build toward personal and family goals through savings features we are developing, subject to relevant authorisations.

03 · INVEST

Invest with visibility

Explore vetted African projects, understand their milestones and follow progress. Future access will depend on eligibility and regulatory approval.

Trust comes first

We know trust has been a challenge. That’s why we’re focused on building confidence through clear due diligence, meaningful safeguards and the information you need to make informed decisions.

Follow the milestones

Our approach is designed around documented project milestones and oversight, with funding released only after each agreed phase has been reviewed and confirmed.

See the bigger picture

Explore project details, follow visible progress updates and ask the questions that matter before making decisions.
