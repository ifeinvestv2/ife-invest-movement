WHAT IS IFE?

Three connected ways to use your money.

IFE means love in Yoruba. (I)ndustry · (F)inance · (E)quity describes how we bring that purpose to life. Send it, save it, invest it — without leaving the ecosystem.

➤
SEND

Send money to yourself

Remit to your own IFE wallet, then choose your next step. Wallet funding is simulated today; live payment and FX partners are planned.

♧
SAVE

Hold it until you're ready

Set money aside while you learn and decide. Interest-bearing savings are planned, subject to an approved regulated provider; demo savings do not earn live interest.

↗
INVEST

Put it into selected opportunities

Explore curated African projects, from smaller and shorter to longer-term, and follow their progress.
